Quran Audio Information

Everything you need to know about Qaris and Quran audio on this website.

🎧
Different Qaris
This website provides Quran recitations from different Qaris. You can select a Qari from the audio menu before playing a Surah.
📖
Qari Availability
Not every Qari has audio available for every Surah. Some Qaris may have many recordings, while others may have recordings for only certain Surahs.
🔄
If Audio Does Not Work
If the selected Qari does not play the current Surah, choose another Qari from the dropdown menu and press the Play button again.
🌐
Internet Connection
Quran audio is streamed online. A stable internet connection is needed to load and play the audio.
▶️
Browser Autoplay
Some browsers do not allow websites to start audio automatically. If automatic playback is blocked, press the Play button on the audio player.
⚡
Automatic Audio
When you open a Surah, the website tries to load the currently selected Qari automatically. Your browser may still require you to press Play.
🎵
Audio Changes With Surah
When you move to another Surah, the audio URL changes automatically to the new Surah for the selected Qari.
💾
Audio Is Streamed
The audio files are not stored directly inside this website. They are streamed from an online Quran audio service.
🔎
Search Any Surah
Use the search box to quickly find a Surah by its English name, translation, Arabic name, or Surah number.
📱
Mobile Friendly
The Quran reader and information panel are designed to work on mobile phones, tablets, and desktop computers.
💡 Helpful Tip
If one Qari does not work for a particular Surah, simply select another Qari. Different Qaris can have different Surah availability.
Quran text, translations, Qari information and audio require an internet connection. Audio availability depends on the online service providing the recording.
