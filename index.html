🕌 Namaz / Salah

Basic information about daily prayer

🕋 Five Daily Prayers

  • Fajr — Morning prayer
  • Dhuhr — Afternoon prayer
  • Asr — Late afternoon prayer
  • Maghrib — Prayer after sunset
  • Isha — Night prayer

🤲 Important Dua

رَبِّ اغْفِرْ لِي وَارْحَمْنِي

My Lord, forgive me and have mercy upon me.

📿 Before Namaz

Make sure you have performed Wudu, your clothes and place are clean, and face the Qiblah.

🕌 Main Steps

  1. Make intention for the prayer.
  2. Stand facing the Qiblah.
  3. Begin the prayer with Takbir.
  4. Recite the required Quranic passages.
  5. Perform Ruku and Sujood.
  6. Complete the required Rakahs.
  7. Finish with Salam.

🌙 Intention

🤝 Helping Others
